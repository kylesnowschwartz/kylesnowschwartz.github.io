LEAGUE RULES

The Sacred Laws of the League

  1. Pay Your Dues League fees must be paid before the draft. No pay, no play. The commissioner will hunt you down.
  2. Set Your Lineup There is no excuse for an empty roster spot. If you can't be bothered, you will be publicly shamed.
  3. No Collusion Trades must be fair. If two teams are caught conspiring, both will face consequences determined by league vote.
  4. Trade Deadline All trades must be completed by Week 10. No exceptions, no matter how much you beg.
  5. Respect the Commish Even when they're the worst. Especially when they're the worst.
  6. Trash Talk Encouraged Keep it fun, keep it creative, keep it in the group chat. What happens in the league stays in the league.
